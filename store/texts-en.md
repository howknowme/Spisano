# Spisano — store listing (English)

## Title
Spisano: Subscription Tracker

## Short description (up to 80 characters)
All subscriptions and bills in one place. Reminders before charges. No sign-up.

## Full description
How much do your subscriptions really cost? Streaming, music, cloud storage, phone, internet, gym, AI tools… Each one is small, but together they add up to a lot every year. And free trials quietly turn into paid plans.

Spisano shows the real total, reminds you before you are charged and helps you decide what to cancel.

PRIVATE
• No sign-up, no accounts
• No bank connection, no access to SMS or email
• All data stays on your phone
• No ads, no analytics
• Works offline

ESSENTIALS
• Monthly, yearly and daily totals with currency conversion
• Subscription budget with usage and an over-budget alert
• “You could save”: mark what you want to cancel and see the yearly savings
• Reminders at the time you choose, even when the app is closed

HANDY
• Free trials: not counted until they end, with a reminder the day before
• Family plans: split with N people, only your share is counted
• Manual bills (rent, utilities, loans): mark as paid, see unpaid and overdue
• Any schedule: every N days, weeks, months or years
• Calendar of all charges and a 12-month forecast
• Spending by category and your most expensive subscriptions
• Payment history and all-time spend
• Quick add for popular services

YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL
• Backup to a file and restore on a new phone
• CSV export for Excel and Google Sheets
• Export to Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar

Dark theme, English and Russian.

Spisano is free and open source: github.com/howknowme/Spisano
